ABOUT HIS LIFE

About Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He came from a simple family and worked hard to achieve his dreams.

He studied aerospace engineering and later worked with the Defence Research and Development Organisation and the Indian Space Research Organisation.

He played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes. Because of his contribution to missile technology, he was popularly known as the Missile Man of India.

In 2002, he became the 11th President of India. He was loved by students because of his simple lifestyle, kind nature and inspiring words.

Dr. Kalam passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture to students. He continues to inspire people across the world.
