Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

1931 – 2015

“Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action.”

Why He Is Remembered

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and leaders. He inspired millions of students to dream big, work hard and serve the nation.

Scientist

Contributed to India's space and missile development programmes.

President

Served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

Teacher

Motivated students to develop knowledge, confidence and strong dreams.
